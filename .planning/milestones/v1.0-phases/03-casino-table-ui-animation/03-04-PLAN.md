**As a** poker learner, **I want to** see the turn card land and an opponent's hand turn over in front of me, **so that** each new piece of information arrives as a visible event I can follow rather than as a number that silently changed.

Purpose: dealing was the first half of the choreography; these three are the transitions the user drives dozens of times per session.
