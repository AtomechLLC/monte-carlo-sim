## Phase Goal

**As a** poker learner, **I want to** sit at a felt table with three opponents across from me, **so that** the odds I am reading feel like they belong to a real hand in play rather than to a calculator form.

Purpose: this is the phase's structural centrepiece. Every later animation plan positions cards against the geometry established here.

Output: `TableScene`, `Seat` and `OddsPanel` components, a recomposed `App`, and the felt palette / geometry CSS — with all 120 existing tests still green apart from two documented adjustments.
</objective>
