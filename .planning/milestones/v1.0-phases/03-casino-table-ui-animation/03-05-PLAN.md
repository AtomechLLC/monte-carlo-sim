| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-16 | Denial of service | Reduced-motion users blocked behind a gate that never opens | mitigate | The arm/release pair is independent of card registration, so the gate resolves immediately with zero-duration animations; asserted by a test that a full navigation sequence ends with the counter at 0 under the suite's forced reduced-motion environment (D-09). |
| T-03-17 | Denial of service | Re-deal during flight stranding the counter or orphaning card elements | mitigate | Slot-plus-`dealNonce` keys force a full remount; `useAnimationGate` releases on unmount; asserted by DOM-shape and counter tests (D-10). |
