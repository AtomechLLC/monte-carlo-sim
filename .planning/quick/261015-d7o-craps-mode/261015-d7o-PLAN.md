**Branch:** `main` (direct, no worktree — `branching_strategy: none`)
**Baseline:** `137e781` (end of 261015-ch8), 81 files / 1435 tests green
**Typecheck gate:** `npx tsc -b`

## Objective

The user's follow-up to the roulette request: "add craps after as a 4th mode." Same shape as
roulette — pick a prediction, run 100 trials, see how often it came in against the true odds —
on the game where the prediction is a BET that can take several rolls to resolve, and where the
probability lesson underneath everything is the dice-total triangle (six ways to roll a 7, one
way to roll a 2).

## Design decisions

1. **A trial is one resolved bet, not one roll.** Pass line, Don't pass and the Place bets ride
   through a come-out and a point; Field, Any seven and Any craps settle on one roll. "Roll 100"
   resolves 100 decisions and shows every die thrown along the way, so the dice-total histogram
   accumulates over hundreds of rolls while the bet's hit rate accumulates over the 100.

2. **Seven bets, the ones a first-timer meets:** Pass line (1:1), Don't pass (1:1, bar 12 — a
   come-out 12 is a PUSH, the one bet with a third outcome), Field (3/4/9/10/11 pay 1:1, 2 pays
   double, 12 pays triple), Any seven (4:1), Any craps (7:1), Place 6 and Place 8 (7:6, always
   working). Every closed-form win probability and expected value is a named constant in the
   engine, each pinned by exact-value tests, seeded anchors, and scripted-roll resolutions.

3. **The Field's 12 is the explorable variable.** Where roulette toggles the wheel and the card
   games toggle the shoe, craps toggles the house rule most tables actually vary: 12 paying
   triple (edge 2.78%) or double (5.56%). It lives in the session bar like the others, applies to
   the Field bet only, and discards a Field history when it changes.

4. **Reuse, not a second copy.** The batch-reveal hook, the observed-vs-true gauge and the
   scoring helper from 261015-ch8 are consumed as-is; `predictionScore` gains a `pushes` count
   and a tallied-outcomes entry point so a bet with pushes or a variable payout (the Field)
   reports the same five readouts. `formatSignedUnits` learns a one-decimal form for the 7:6
   place payout. The dice-total table reuses the Share column's relative-to-max bars.

5. **The felt is the classic layout, read-only art except for the bets.** PASS LINE along the
   bottom, DON'T PASS above it, the FIELD band with its numbers, the six place boxes across the
   top (4 5 6 8 9 10, with 6 and 8 the pressable ones), the proposition boxes for Any seven and
   Any craps; two SVG dice with real pips show the last roll, and the last trial's roll sequence
   is spelled out beneath them. Rounded rectangle, like roulette.

6. **Changing the bet clears the history.** Unlike roulette, a craps trial's boundaries depend on
   the bet (a pass-line decision spans rolls), so a history cannot be honestly re-scored under a
   different bet.

7. **Shell discipline, fourth time.** `GameMode` gains `'craps'`; the switcher a fourth segment
   (the control-placement census moves 5 → 6, cited); the mode-shell guard is amended
   additively; the craps tree reads only `crapsStore`.
