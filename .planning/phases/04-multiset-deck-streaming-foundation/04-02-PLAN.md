## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 4 goal (engine/store-only, no user-visible surface): *"The deck/shoe model correctly represents 1 or 2 physical decks — two copies of the same card are distinct, trackable objects that never collapse via value-based dedup — and the simulation streaming pipeline is generalized to serve any game, with zero behavioral drift from v1.0 at deckCount=1."*

<mvp_note>
Contract-only plan, sanctioned engine-foundation exception (see 04-01's `<mvp_note>`). This plan produces the single load-bearing primitive that Phases 6, 7 and 8 all consume; PITFALLS.md Pitfall 6 rates it "foundational — must land before ANY other multi-deck work" and rates retro-fitting it after the fact as HIGH recovery cost. There is no thinner vertical slice available: the shoe is the data model, and no user-visible deck-count behaviour can exist until it does. The user-visible payoff arrives in Phase 6 (Blackjack deck toggle) and Phase 8 (cross-game toggle).
</mvp_note>

<objective>
Add `src/engine/shoe.ts`: a new, additive engine module providing count-aware multiset deck construction and subtraction for 1 or 2 physical decks, with `src/engine/cards.ts` left completely untouched.

Purpose: DECK-01 and roadmap criterion 2. Every other multiset change in this phase (conditioning's `remainingDeck`, the worker's overlap budget, the picker's block threshold) routes through this one module, so the `Map<Card, number>` occurrence-budget logic is written and property-tested exactly once instead of being re-derived — and mis-derived — in four places, which is precisely the situation ARCHITECTURE.md found in v1 (`cards.ts` and `conditioning.ts` independently duplicating the same Set-based bug).

Output: `shoe.ts` with `DeckCount`, `buildShoe`, `shoeSize`, `cardCounts`, `shoeWithout`, and `shoe.test.ts` proving both the v1 parity invariant and the 2-deck multiset invariants.
</objective>
