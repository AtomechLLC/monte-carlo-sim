## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 4 goal (engine/store-only, no user-visible surface): *"The deck/shoe model correctly represents 1 or 2 physical decks — two copies of the same card are distinct, trackable objects that never collapse via value-based dedup — and the simulation streaming pipeline is generalized to serve any game, with zero behavioral drift from v1.0 at deckCount=1."*

<mvp_note>
Phase-acceptance plan for a sanctioned engine-foundation phase (see 04-01's `<mvp_note>`). The human checkpoint here is deliberately lighter and differently-shaped than prior phases': there is no new feature to tour, because the phase's defining success condition is that the user sees NOTHING new. The walkthrough is therefore a regression sweep — "does the shipped v1.0 experience still work identically after four plans of engine surgery" — plus a review of the parity evidence that backs the claim.
</mvp_note>

<objective>
Close Phase 4 with a permanent source-level guard for DECK-01's "no value-based dedup anywhere in the shoe path", a collated simultaneous pass over every phase gate, and a human confirmation that the running app is unchanged.

Purpose: DECK-01 is partly a source-shape requirement ("no value-based `Set`/`Map` dedup anywhere in the shoe path"), and source-shape requirements decay silently — a future contributor reaches for `new Set(cards)` out of habit and nothing fails. An executable guard converts that requirement from prose in a requirements file into a red test. The same guard pins the artefacts D-07 and D-10 declare untouchable, so a later phase cannot quietly loosen them.

Output: `shoePath.guard.test.ts`, a one-symbol extension to the scoped Node built-in declarations it needs, a collated evidence record, and an approved human regression sweep.
</objective>
