## Phase Goal

**As a** probability explorer using the simulator, **I want to** play a full Blackjack round on its own table — deal, watch bust / dealer-outcome / EV odds converge live, hit or stand, reveal the hole card, and switch the shoe between 1 and 2 decks — **so that** I can see exactly how each new piece of information and each extra deck reshapes the numbers.

<objective>
Build Blackjack's two stores and the symmetric restore-mount signal: round lifecycle (deal / hit / stand / reveal / deck toggle), the streaming odds cache, and the gameModeStore field that keeps a switch-back from replaying Blackjack's choreography.

Purpose: this is where the animation-gate accounting and the dual-exclusion-set discipline become runtime behavior rather than pure functions. Both have already produced real shipped bugs on the Hold'em side (05-REVIEW CR-02, PITFALLS Pitfall 5) and both recur identically for Blackjack if the arm/release balance is not designed in from the first commit.
Output: two new stores with full test coverage, plus one additive field on the only cross-game store.

**Planner decisions taken under Claude's Discretion (06-CONTEXT grants "exact store partitioning for blackjack odds" and D-10 grants the restore-signal call), recorded for audit:**
- **Two stores, not one** (`blackjackStore` for round state, `blackjackOddsStore` for streamed odds), mirroring the shipped `gameStore`/`oddsStore` split. The one-way dependency runs round-state -> odds-store only.
- **Knowledge key is `${playerHandLength}|${revealedHole ? 1 : 0}`.** Within a round, cards are only ever ADDED and the hole is only ever revealed, so those two dimensions fully determine visibility. `deckCount` is deliberately NOT in the key: `setDeckCount` clears the whole cache instead, which is what makes BJ-07's "toggle and watch it re-converge" moment fire on every toggle rather than silently serving a cached arm on the way back (D-12, UI-SPEC A3).
- **`deal()` always arms exactly one gate unit and always bumps `roundNonce`, including when a natural resolves the round immediately.** 06-RESEARCH's deal sketch returns before `beginAnimation()` on the natural path; that would leave `BlackjackTable`'s release effect firing on a `roundNonce` change with nothing armed, which is precisely the CR-02 unit-theft bug class. Arming unconditionally on `deal()` keeps the accounting balanced by construction — the four cards still fly in on a natural-resolved round, so there is a real animation to gate.
</objective>
