  <how-to-verify>
    Start the dev server with `npm run dev` and open the printed local URL.

    1. **Empty state.** Before touching anything, confirm the page shows "No hand dealt yet" with the instruction to click Deal or use the picker.
    2. **Construct a scenario (DEAL-02/DEAL-03).** Click the "Hero 1" slot, pick the Ace of spades. Click "Hero 2", pick the Ace of hearts. Reopen the panel from "Flop 1" and confirm both aces now read "As (used)" / "Ah (used)" and cannot be clicked. Pick three flop cards. Click Deal.
    3. **Picks are honoured and persist.** Confirm your hero hole shows exactly the two aces and the flop slots you set. Click Deal again and confirm the same picked cards come back with fresh opponents. Click "Clear All" and confirm every slot returns to "—".
    4. **Live convergence (carried over from Phase 1).** Click Deal. Confirm the trial counter climbs and the win/tie/lose percentages visibly settle, and that the page stays responsive (scroll, hover buttons) while it does.
    5. **Advance (NAV-01).** Click Advance. Confirm three community cards appear, the street label reads "Flop", and the odds restart from zero trials and converge to a new value. Advance twice more through "Turn" and "River", checking a card is added each time.
    6. **Rewind (NAV-02).** Click Rewind back to "Flop". Confirm the community cards drop back to three, the odds show the flop's settled numbers immediately with no visible re-convergence, and the trial counter does not restart. Advance again to "River" and confirm the exact same cards reappear.
    7. **Reveal (NAV-03).** Rewind to "Flop". Note the current win percentage. Click any opponent seat. Confirm that opponent's two cards appear on the seat, the seat can no longer be clicked, and the odds recompute. Judge whether the shift makes sense: revealing a strong opponent hand should push your win percentage down, a weak one up.
    8. **Reveal persists backwards (D-09).** Rewind to "Pre-Flop". Confirm the revealed opponent's cards are still shown and that the pre-flop odds recompute (trial counter climbs again) rather than snapping back to the pre-reveal numbers.
    9. **Keyboard and focus.** Tab through the page. Confirm every button (slots, Clear, Clear All, Rewind, Advance, opponent seats, the 52 card buttons) is reachable, shows a visible focus ring, and that Escape closes the card panel and returns focus to the slot you opened it from.
    10. **Re-deal resets.** Click Deal. Confirm all opponents are hidden again, the street is back to "Pre-Flop", and the odds recompute from zero rather than showing the previous hand's numbers.

    Stop the dev server when you are done — do not leave it running.
  </how-to-verify>
