## Phase Goal

**As a** poker learner, **I want to** sit at the finished table and see whether it actually feels like a poker table, **so that** the phase ships on the strength of the experience rather than on the strength of its test count.

<objective>
Human acceptance of Phase 3. Everything automatable was automated in 03-01 through 03-05; this plan verifies what a test cannot judge — whether the felt reads as a table, whether the choreography feels snappy rather than sluggish or frantic, whether the flip is a real 3D turn, and whether the odds ever appear to know something the cards have not yet shown.

Purpose: TBL-01 through TBL-03 are perceptual claims and TBL-04's spirit ("the app must never spoil a card") is a judgement about what the user could infer. Only a person watching the real, non-reduced-motion app can accept them.

Output: an approval, or a defect list to close before the phase is marked complete.
</objective>

  <how-to-verify>
    Make sure your operating system's "reduce motion" accessibility setting is OFF for steps 1-9, then start the dev server with `npm run dev` and open the printed local URL.

    1. **The tab.** Before anything else, check the browser tab: the title reads "Monte Carlo Poker Simulator" and the favicon is a purple spade, not a Vite logo (D-14).
    2. **The empty table (TBL-01).** Before clicking anything, confirm the scene reads as a casino table: a green felt oval with a darker rim, your seat bottom-centre labelled "You", three seats across the top labelled "Opponent 1/2/3", five dashed card positions in the middle, and a small deck stack off to the right. Confirm the odds panel (win/tie/loss, trial counter, category table) sits beside or below the felt and never on top of it. Confirm the empty-state message names both Deal and Set Up Scenario.
    3. **The deal (TBL-03).** Click Deal. Watch the eight hole cards: they should fly out of the deck one at a time in dealer rotation — opponent 1, 2, 3, you, then around again — quickly enough to feel snappy but slowly enough to follow. Nothing should overlap oddly or land in the wrong seat.
    4. **The gate (TBL-04).** During that deal, watch the odds panel. Every percentage and the trial counter must show an em dash while cards are moving, and the numbers must only start appearing and converging once the last card has landed. At no point should a number flicker into view mid-deal.
    5. **The card art (TBL-02).** Look closely at your two cards: pips should be countable and correctly laid out, court cards should look like proper court cards, and corner indices should be legible at the rendered size. Confirm all six opponent cards show the same card back, and that the back's colour reads as part of this app rather than an arbitrary red or blue.
    6. **Street advance (TBL-03/TBL-04).** Click Advance. Confirm exactly three flop cards fly in from the deck and that the two cards already on the felt do not move or re-animate. Confirm the odds show em dashes until the last flop card lands, then converge. Advance twice more and confirm only one new card animates each time.
    7. **Rewind (TBL-04/D-12).** Click Rewind. Confirm the departing card fades and slides away rather than vanishing instantly, and — this is the important one — that the earlier street's settled odds only reappear *after* the card has finished leaving, not while it is still fading.
    8. **Reveal (TBL-03).** Click any opponent seat. Confirm both of that opponent's cards turn over together with a genuine 3D flip (you should see the card edge-on mid-turn, not a flat fade or a squash), that the seat does not change size during the flip, and that the odds stay dashed until the flip finishes and only then recompute.
    9. **Re-deal mid-animation (D-10).** Click Deal, and while cards are still flying, click Deal again immediately. Confirm the first choreography is abandoned cleanly: no leftover or duplicated cards on the felt, no card morphing into another mid-air, and the odds still resolve correctly for the new hand.
    10. **Reduced motion (D-09).** Turn ON your operating system's "reduce motion" setting and reload. Repeat a deal, an advance and a reveal. Confirm every card appears directly in its final position with no movement, and that the odds still compute correctly rather than hanging on em dashes forever. Turn the setting back off when done.
    11. **Keyboard and focus.** With motion back on, Tab through the page. Confirm Deal, Set Up Scenario, Rewind, Advance and all three opponent seats are reachable, each shows a visible focus ring, and each has a comfortable click target. Open Set Up Scenario and confirm the Phase 2 picker still works exactly as it did, including Escape closing the 52-card panel.
    12. **No console noise.** Open the browser devtools console and confirm no errors or React warnings were logged during any of the above.

    Stop the dev server when you are done — do not leave it running.
