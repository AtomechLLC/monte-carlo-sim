## Phase Goal

**As a** probability explorer using the simulator, **I want to** play Hold'em over a 104-card two-deck shoe — with duplicate-card hands evaluated correctly, a Five of a Kind row in the odds table, and a visible cue on the second copy of any card on the felt — **so that** I can see exactly how deck composition changes what hands are possible and how likely they are.

<objective>
Wire the duplicate-aware evaluator into the live trial path and grow the category histogram to eleven entries wherever — and only where — `deckCount === 2` flows, then retire WR-03 at the worker boundary.

Purpose: this is the category-index spine. 07-PATTERNS trap 2 establishes that `protocol.ts`, `equity.ts`, `simulationApi.ts` and `oddsStore.ts` share ONE snapshot-shape contract; splitting them across concurrent plans risks a length-10-versus-11 mismatch mid-wave and dev-guard console spam. They land together, in one plan, in dependency order. The same plan carries the D-07/WR-04 guard extension, because the new wrapper module must join the shoe-path prohibition lists the moment it becomes reachable from the trial path.
Output: four surgical production edits, two new sibling test files, one retargeted acceptance test and one extended source-shape guard. No UI, no stores beyond the odds dev-guard, no new snapshot fields.

**Planner decisions taken under Claude's Discretion, recorded here for audit:**
- `categoryCountFor` and `FIVE_OF_A_KIND_INDEX` live in `protocol.ts`, not in the new engine wrapper. `protocol.ts` is already the value-exporting side of the protocol-to-engine import direction (its lines 1-3 document the cycle it exists to avoid), and `equity.ts` already imports `CATEGORY_COUNT` from it; putting the derived constants anywhere else creates either a runtime cycle or a second source of the number 10.
- The D-13 3-sigma statistical anchor stays in plan 07-01 at the evaluator level. This plan asserts the INTEGRATION instead — that index 10 exists, tallies, survives the merge, survives the snapshot copy and reconciles to `trialsCompleted` — at a trial count cheap enough to run in the normal suite.
</objective>
