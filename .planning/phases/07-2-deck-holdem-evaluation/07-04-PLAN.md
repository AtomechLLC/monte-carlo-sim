## Phase Goal

**As a** probability explorer using the simulator, **I want to** play Hold'em over a 104-card two-deck shoe — with duplicate-card hands evaluated correctly, a Five of a Kind row in the odds table, and a visible cue on the second copy of any card on the felt — **so that** I can see exactly how deck composition changes what hands are possible and how likely they are.

<objective>
Make a duplicate on the felt read as a deliberate table fact the instant it first appears, by badging the second visible copy of any duplicated card with a small corner cue that rides the card through every animation.

Purpose: HE2-03 exists because a second `Ah` on the table looks exactly like a rendering bug. This plan is the whole visible payoff of the 2-deck shoe on the felt, and it is also the plan most exposed to the phase's subtlest trap: felt React keys are positional precisely so two identical cards cannot collide, and nothing here may disturb them.
Output: one pure derivation module with headless vectors, an optional badge on the card component, and the threading through the three render paths that carry face-up cards — plus render tests pinning DOM-absence at one deck in both directions.

**Planner decisions taken under Claude's Discretion (07-CONTEXT allows the cue's exact treatment within tokens and the mechanism), recorded here for audit:**
- The badge is an optional `copyCue` prop on `PlayingCard`, rendered as a fragment sibling of the shipped `<img>`. When the prop is absent or false, `PlayingCard` returns the shipped `<img>` unchanged, so the 1-deck DOM is byte-identical rather than merely visually identical.
- The derivation lives in its own module with a `gameStore`-subscribing hook, so `HandDisplay` and `BoardDisplay` consume one memoised result instead of each re-deriving the scan.
- Slot keys are composed by exported helpers (`heroCueKey`/`communityCueKey`/`opponentCueKey`) rather than hand-built template strings at each call site — the same single-bridge discipline `PlayingCard`'s card-code-to-art mapping already enforces.
- **This plan writes NO CSS.** `.copy-cue` and `.card-slot--cued` are emitted here as a binding class-name contract and styled by plan 07-05, which owns `App.css` for the whole phase (the 06-05/06-06/06-07 precedent, where the odds and controls plans emitted class names and wrote no CSS of their own).
</objective>
